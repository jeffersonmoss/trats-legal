Trats is a bilingual (English and Spanish) job-management app for field-service businesses.

Contact

Email support@trats.app with your question, the email address you sign in with, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common Questions

  • How do I add my team? In Settings, share your company's join code. A teammate creates an account and enters the code to join your company.
  • Why does a text open my phone's texting app? Trats does not send texts itself. It saves the message, in the customer's language, to the customer's record and opens it in your texting app so it goes out from your own number.
  • How do I turn AI translation on or off? Settings, Privacy and AI.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings, Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is deleted.
