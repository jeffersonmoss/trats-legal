Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trats ("Trats," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares and deletes information in the Trats app.

Who We Are

Trats is a bilingual (English and Spanish) job-management app for field-service businesses, such as roofing, painting, cleaning, landscaping and catering companies. A business and its team use Trats to keep track of customers, jobs, estimates, schedules and their conversations with customers.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, preferred language and, if you add it, your phone number, plus the company you belong to and your role in it.
  • Business records you or your team enter: customer names, phone numbers, email addresses and service addresses; leads, jobs, estimates, change orders, invoices, payments, schedules, notes, tasks, messages and job photos.
  • Voice input: when you dictate, your phone's own speech recognition service (Apple or Google) turns speech into text. Trats keeps the resulting text only where you save it, and keeps a transcript of an interpreter session only if you choose to save it.
  • Technical information needed to sign you in and keep your devices in sync, such as session tokens. Trats has no advertising, analytics or tracking software and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How We Use Information

  • To run the app for you and your team: storing your company's records, syncing them between your devices, and producing estimates, invoices and job summaries.
  • To translate between English and Spanish, and for the other AI features below, only if you allow it.
  • To send account emails such as sign-in codes and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure and to comply with the law.

AI Features and OpenRouter

Translation, dictation commands, daily-log summaries and review sorting send the text involved, including any names, addresses or phone numbers in it, to OpenRouter, a third-party AI service, which passes it to an AI model to process and returns the result. Trats asks for your permission in the app before anything is sent, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings, under Privacy and AI. With it off, Trats still works, without automatic translation.

Text Messages to Customers

Trats does not send text messages itself. When you text a customer, Trats saves the message to the customer's record and opens it in your phone's own texting app, and you send it from your own number. Your carrier's terms apply to those messages. You are responsible for having your customers' permission to text them.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing or advertising.

We share information only:

  • Within your company: members of your company can see its records.
  • With service providers that run parts of the service for us, only as needed: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), OpenRouter and the AI model it routes to (only with your permission, as described above), Resend (account emails), and Open-Meteo (weather forecasts, which receive only the city you choose for weather, never your personal information).
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of people.

Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account in the app: open Settings and tap Delete account.

  • If you are the only person in your company, the company is deleted with all of its records and photos.
  • If others remain, your sign-in and profile are deleted. Records you created stay with your company, without your name attached. If you were the company's only owner, another member becomes the owner.

Deletion takes effect immediately. If you are a customer of a business that uses Trats and want your information removed, ask that business, or contact us below.

Data Retention

We keep your company's records until your company deletes them or deletes its account.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and each company can reach only its own records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Trats is a business tool. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the most recent change.
